Post your short videos to TikTok, on your schedule.

White Hole Publisher is a simple web tool for creators who make short videos. Connect your TikTok account, pick a video, write your caption, choose who can see it, and post it now or schedule it for later.

Open the app How it works
1

Connect

Log in with TikTok. You always see which account you are posting to.

2

Pick a video

Choose from your video library or upload one from your device, and preview it.

3

Set the details

Edit the caption, choose the audience, and decide on comments, Duet and Stitch.

4

Post or schedule

Post right away or pick a date and time. Track the status of every post.

White Hole Publisher never posts anything without your explicit confirmation, never adds watermarks to your videos, and only stores what it needs to post on your behalf. You can disconnect at any time from the app or from TikTok's settings. Read our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
