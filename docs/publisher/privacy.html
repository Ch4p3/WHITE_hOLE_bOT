Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

White Hole Publisher ("we", "the app") is a web tool that lets creators post and schedule their own videos to their TikTok account. This policy explains what information the app uses and how.

Information we access from TikTok

  • Basic profile info (scope user.info.basic): your TikTok open ID, display name and avatar, used only to show you which account you are posting to.
  • Posting permission (scope video.publish): used only to post the videos you choose, with the caption, privacy level and interaction settings you select, when you press Post or Schedule.
  • Creator settings and post status returned by TikTok (available privacy levels, comment/Duet/Stitch settings, maximum video length, processing status) so the posting screen matches your account.

What we store

  • Your TikTok access and refresh tokens and your open ID, kept on our private server (never in your browser and never shared), so we can complete the posts you confirm, including scheduled posts.
  • Your scheduled posts (video link, caption, chosen settings and time) until they are posted, plus a short history for up to 7 days so you can see their status.
  • Your browser keeps only a random session identifier.

We do not collect your TikTok videos' analytics, followers, messages or any other data, and we do not sell, rent or share your information with third parties. Videos you upload from your device are sent directly from your browser to TikTok and are not stored by us.

Deleting your data

Click Disconnect in the app to revoke our access and immediately delete your tokens and scheduled posts. You can also remove the app from TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions. To request deletion by email, write to 4pisocyt@gmail.com.

Security

The TikTok client secret and your tokens are stored only server-side with restricted access. All traffic uses HTTPS.

Children

The app is not intended for anyone under 18 years old.

Changes and contact

We will post any changes on this page. Questions: 4pisocyt@gmail.com.
